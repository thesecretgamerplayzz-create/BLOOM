Bloom



Bloom is an AI powered mental health companion designed for students It gives students a friendly space to pause reflect and reset through daily check ins calming tools goal tracking AI conversations and helpful resources



Features



Daily Mood Check In



Track how you are feeling each day and build awareness of your emotions over time



AI Chatbot Buddy



Talk with an AI companion about thoughts problems school stress and everyday challenges



Calm Down



Use calming tools and guided breathing exercises when you need a moment to relax



Wind Down



Follow relaxing evening prompts and routines to help you slow down and prepare for rest



Goals



Create small achievable goals and track your progress as you build positive habits



Resources



Access helpful mental health resources designed to help students find additional support



Who It Is For



Bloom is made for students dealing with school stress deadlines goals and everyday challenges



It is designed to be a lightweight companion that fits naturally into daily life



Bloom is not a replacement for professional mental health care

What does Bloom actually do



Bloom is an AI powered mental health companion made for students It lets users check in with their mood talk with an AI buddy use calming and breathing exercises set personal goals and find helpful mental health resources



What was the hardest part to build



The hardest part was bringing all the different features together into one simple experience while keeping the website easy to use and responsive The AI chatbot and creating a smooth flow between the different tools were also challenging



What would you still want to add or fix



I would like to improve the AI companion make conversations more personalized and add better mood tracking over time I would also like to add more calming activities more useful student resources and improve the overall performance and accessibility



Anything you learned building it



I learned how to structure a complete web application and connect different features together using HTML CSS and JavaScript I also learned how important it is to design for real users and make an app feel simple approachable and genuinely useful
