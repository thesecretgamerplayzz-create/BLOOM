🌱 Bloom — Your Mental Health Buddy

Bloom is an AI-powered mental health companion website built for students. It offers daily check-ins, calming tools, goal tracking, and easy access to helpful resources — a friendly space to pause, reflect, and reset, right in the browser.

🔗 Live Site: https://bloom-chi-ten.vercel.app

✨ Features
How Are You Feeling Today? — The landing page of Bloom. A simple daily mood check-in that helps track how you're doing over time.
AI Chatbot Buddy — Talk things through with an AI companion that listens, helps you process your thoughts, and works through problems with you.
Calm Down — Guided tools to help you de-stress in the moment, including breathing exercises.
Wind Down — Evening routines and prompts to help you relax and prepare for rest.
Goals — Set and track small, achievable goals to build healthy habits over time.
Resources — Curated mental health resources tailored for students, including where to find further support.
🎯 Who It's For

Bloom is built with students in mind — navigating school, stress, deadlines, and everything in between. It's a lightweight companion that fits into daily life, not a replacement for professional care.
